System Design

HLD (High-Level Design)
Overall architecture of the system.
Focuses on major components and communication.
Includes: services, APIs, databases, load balancer, cache.
Mostly language-independent.

LLD (Low-Level Design)A
Detailed design of individual components.
Focuses on classes, objects, methods, interfaces.
Includes OOP, SOLID principles, design patterns, UML.
More language-dependent.

HLD				LLD
Overall system			Individual components
Architecture			Implementation
Services & APIs			Classes & methods
Scalability			SOLID & Design Patterns
Mostly language-independent	More language-dependent
